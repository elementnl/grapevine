# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Casual daily-game players, the Wordle and Krillion crowd. They open Grapevine for a couple of minutes, usually on a phone, take their turns, and come back the next day to see where their drawings went. Many arrive from a link a friend shared.

## Product Purpose

A daily picture-telephone game where everyone grows one shared tree. Each turn hands you a drawing to guess or a guess to draw; the next day the frozen tree is revealed, showing how one prompt drifted down every branch. Success is the reveal being funny enough to share, and the turn being quick and pleasant enough to come back to tomorrow.

## Brand Personality

Playful, hand-made, warm. It should feel like a shared whiteboard someone doodled on: sketchy ink strokes, a hand-lettered voice, a marker accent or two. Fun without being loud; the drawings are the stars, the interface is the margin around them.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS: clean cards, gray hairline borders, rounded pill buttons.
- Overdone skeuomorphism: photo-real paper, leather, heavy textures.
- Childish: cartoon mascots, babyish colors, sticker overload.
- Dark mode as the default: this is paper-first.

## Design Principles

- **The drawings are the content.** Chrome stays quiet and sketchy so players' drawings carry the screen.
- **One medium.** Every surface, control and connector looks like it was drawn on the same whiteboard as the drawings.
- **Quick turns.** A turn is one clear task with one obvious action, readable at a glance on a phone.
- **The reveal is the payoff.** The tree view is something to explore and share, not a list to scroll.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast at least 4.5:1 even with the hand-drawn look, visible focus on every control, full keyboard support for the tree canvas (pan and zoom without a mouse), and reduced-motion alternatives for every animation. Hand-lettered type is for headings and labels; body copy and inputs stay highly legible.
